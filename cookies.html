Cookie Policy

What cookies we use, why, and how you can control them.

Last updated: May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Calon AI Solutions Ltd ("Calon", "we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website at calonaisolutions.com. We are registered in England and Wales (Company No. 15984397).

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or actions. Cookies cannot harm your device and do not contain personally identifiable information on their own.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as filling in a form or setting preferences. You can set your browser to block these cookies, but some parts of the site may not work as a result.

Analytics cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and understand how visitors move around our website, so we can measure and improve performance. The information collected is aggregated and anonymous — we cannot identify you from this data.

We may use tools such as Google Analytics or similar privacy-focused analytics platforms. Where we do, these tools may set their own cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking via the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Functional cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalisation, such as remembering your preferences. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we use on our pages.

Marketing cookies

We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies on this website. If this changes, we will update this policy and seek your consent where required.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some pages on our website may include content from third-party services (such as embedded maps or social media buttons) that may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies. Please refer to the relevant third parties' privacy and cookie policies for more information.

4. How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

Browser settings

Most browsers allow you to view, manage, delete, and block cookies. Note that deleting or blocking cookies may affect your experience on some websites. To find out how to manage cookies in your browser, visit:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Opt-out tools

You can opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies through the Your Online Choices website.

5. Your Consent

When you first visit our website, we will ask for your consent to set non-essential cookies. You can withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or contacting us at hello@calonaisolutions.com.

Strictly necessary cookies do not require your consent as they are essential for the website to function.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or regulation. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised.
